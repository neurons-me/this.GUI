## Namespace Principles

1. **Namespace Immutability:** Once a namespace is defined, its semantic purpose must not change. If the logic evolves drastically, a new namespace is created. This prevents “logical drift” over time.
1. **Selector Hierarchy:** The selector acts as the DNA of the path. It is not just a label—it is the filter that determines which information is valid within that branch of the fractal.
1. **Conflict Resolution:** By removing the rigidity of a “constant,” the protocol needs a precedence rule. If two prefixes touch the same namespace, which one has authority over the path?
